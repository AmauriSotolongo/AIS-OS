Did you know Steve Jobs and Jeff Bezos both pointed to one business book as the one that influenced them the most, and it was the same book. So what was the book? The Innovator's Dilemma, written by Harvard professor in 1997, and it makes one argument. Big companies lose to cheaper and simpler products from smaller companies because the big companies protect the things that are already making them money. But the better way to survive is to build the product that replaces what you currently offer before somebody else does. Bezos built the Kindle knowing it would take sales away from Amazon's own paper books, and Steve Jobs shipped the iPhone knowing it would replace the iPod. That is why The Innovator's Dilemma is the one book that Jobs and Bezos both agreed on. Follow for more.
